How Lostly works

Short answers to the questions students actually ask.

I lost something. What do I do?

Check the board first — search by the item name and filter by the place you last had it. If it is not there, report it as lost with a clear description and the date. Anyone who finds a matching item will see your report, and you will get an alert when a possible match is posted.

I found something. Should I hand it to security?

Either is fine. If you post it here, do not include a detail that only the owner would know (serial number, what is inside). Keep that for the private details field — it is never shown publicly and is used only to check a claim.

How does claiming work?

Open the listing and choose This is mine. Describe something only the owner would know. The person who posted the item sees your message and can approve it, decline it, or ask for more detail. Nobody else can read it. When a claim is approved the listing becomes Returned and the other claims on it are closed.

Where should we meet?

In a public place on campus during daylight — the library help desk, the cafeteria, a security counter. Never share OTPs, bank details or pay a "release fee"; nobody at the university will ask for one.

Someone posted my item but will not reply. What now?

Use Report this listing on the item page and say briefly what is wrong. An administrator sees the flag in the moderation queue and can step in, including hiding the listing.

Why can't I see my old listing?

Listings are archived automatically after 90 days so the board stays useful. Your own listings remain visible in your dashboard whatever their status, and an administrator can restore anything archived by mistake.

What can an administrator see?

Moderators can see every listing, every claim decision, account details and the audit log of who changed what and why. They cannot read your password — it is stored only as a bcrypt hash — and they cannot read the private details of a listing you did not post.
