How it works

Four steps from lost to found

Report an item
Step 1

Report it

Post the item with its category, campus location and date. Add a photo, and keep any identifying details private.

Step 2

We match it

Every new report is compared with open listings of the opposite type. Both people are notified when keywords line up.

Step 3

Claim it

Submit a private claim with proof only the owner would know. Nothing is published publicly.

Step 4

Get it back

The finder approves the claim, the listing is marked returned, and the handover happens in a public campus spot.

Stay safe

Meet at the library entrance, the cafeteria or the security desk — never a private address. Campus security can hold high-value items. Never share OTPs, bank details or payment requests, and do not post ID numbers or room numbers in a public listing.
