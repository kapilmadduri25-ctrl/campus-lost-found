Privacy

What this portal stores, who can see it, and how long it stays.

What is stored

  • Your account: name, college email, hashed password, optional department and phone.
  • Your listings: the public description, category, place, date, optional photo, plus the private details you chose to add.
  • Claims: the message and proof you send to the person holding the item.
  • Audit trail: who moderated or changed what, when, and the reason given.

What other students can see

Your display name, the listing details and its status. They never see your email address, your phone number, your department, the private details of a listing, or the contents of any claim you did not make.

What administrators can see

Everything above, plus the moderation history, so they can resolve disputes and act on reports. Passwords are stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash — nobody, including an administrator, can read them.

How long it is kept

Open listings are archived automatically after 90 days. Resolved listings stay for the record until deleted. Password reset tokens expire after 30 minutes and are stored only as a hash. You can ask an administrator to delete your account and everything attached to it.

Cookies and storage

There are no advertising or analytics cookies. The browser stores your sign-in token and your light/dark theme preference in localStorage; clearing site data signs you out.

Questions

Speak to the project guide or the campus administrator. This page describes a student project and is not legal advice.
